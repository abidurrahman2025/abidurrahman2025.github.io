Real Estate Calculator

Question: What will a property cost, what can I afford, and does the return make sense?

Approach: One tool with four tabs: mortgage (payment breakdown, interest, payoff date), affordability (maximum home price from income and debts), ROI (future value, net profit, cap rate) and rental yield (gross and net yield, cash flow). Charts show each result.

Tools: HTML, JavaScript, Chart.js. AI-assisted.

Estimates use simplified assumptions and are for exploration, not financial advice.
