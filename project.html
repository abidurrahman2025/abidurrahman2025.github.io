Assessing Automotive Efficiency

Technology gains vs. vehicle size, 1975–2025

The question

Fuel economy has improved a lot since the mid-1970s, but vehicles also changed size and weight. How much of the gain came from better engineering, and how much from building smaller cars?

Approach

I compared efficiency trends with vehicle size across fifty years of data. Reading each model year against the size of the vehicles that year makes the comparison fair between eras.

What it shows

The analysis estimates how much of the improvement would have appeared at a constant vehicle size, which is the core of an honest comparison across decades.

Bank Telemarketing Campaign Optimization

Predictive modeling and customer segmentation for term deposit conversion

The question

Phone campaigns are expensive, and most calls don't end in a subscription. Which customers are worth calling, and how do we know?

Approach

  • Explored customer attributes (age, job, balance, prior contact) to find what separates people who subscribe from those who don't.
  • Built predictive models to score each customer's likelihood of subscribing.
  • Segmented customers into groups with similar profiles, so messages can be tailored to each one.

What it shows

Modeling turns a broad calling list into a ranked one. The segments make the result usable by a marketing team, not just a score.

Real Estate Calculator

Mortgage, affordability, investment ROI and rental yield in one tool

The question

Buying a home or an investment property involves several separate calculations: the monthly payment, how much you can afford, the return, and whether rent covers the costs.

What it does

  • Mortgage: monthly principal and interest, tax, insurance and PMI, with total interest, payoff date and a balance chart.
  • Affordability: the highest price your income, debts and debt-to-income limit support.
  • Investment ROI: a year-by-year projection with rent and expense growth, financing, appreciation and selling costs. It reports an annualized return.
  • Rental yield: gross and net yield, vacancy loss, and cash flow after the mortgage.
  • Loan schedule: a yearly summary and a downloadable monthly schedule.

Design choices

All tabs share one set of assumptions, so changing the interest rate updates every calculation. The calculation logic is tested against known values, and any link can be shared with the inputs already filled in.

Estimates use simplified assumptions and are for exploration, not financial advice.
