About me

Hello, I'm Abidur

An Information Science student who wants to make data work for people.

My story

I'm a senior undergraduate at the University of South Florida, Tampa Campus, studying Information Science with a concentration in Data Science and Analysis.

I started with spreadsheets and Access databases, then moved into Python and R to work with larger datasets and build models. Today I use Python, R and Tableau to turn raw numbers into answers a decision-maker can act on.

I enjoy projects where the question matters as much as the code: why fuel economy has changed, which bank customers are worth calling, or whether a rental property pays off. I also build software in Java, from group analytics projects to independent applications.

I'm passionate about using AI to write smarter code, while staying hands-on with every step of the data lifecycle.

My interests

Web development

Machine learning

Predictive models, evaluated honestly and tied to a real decision.
