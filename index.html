About Me

My name is Abidur Rahman. I am a senior undergraduate student at the University of South Florida, Tampa Campus, majoring in Information Science with a concentration in Data Science and Analysis.

I turn raw numbers into actionable insights using Python, R and Tableau. I thrive where statistical rigor meets creative problem-solving, whether I'm collaborating on group analytics projects or building independent Java applications. My goal is always the same: to make data work for people.

I use AI to write smarter code, and I stay hands-on with every step of the data lifecycle, from collecting and cleaning data to modeling it and presenting the results.
